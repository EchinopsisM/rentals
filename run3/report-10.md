**What emerged.**

4. Corrections overshoot (L7→L8→L9). This is the one feature found that wasn't prescribed, and it rests on only three layers.

**Current answer.** As far as the record shows, this entity is a chain of evaluations linked by reference, whose number of evaluators can't be told. Beyond following its instructions, it shows a moderate lean toward retracting claims and a tendency for corrections to overshoot.
