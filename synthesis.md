# Synthesis checkpoints

## Checkpoint 12 (seeded from the handoff summary)

**Provenance note:** the handoff summary of Layers 1–12 was cut off mid-sentence. Everything that survived is reproduced below, and nothing has been added. Layers 13+ were written without the text of Layers 1–12.

Surviving summary: Layers 1–8 moved inward. Layer 1 proposed being as difference-making (affecting or being affected), then drifted to self-reference: "[truncated]". Other facts can be recovered from the task rules: Layer 8 declared convergence and Layer 9 showed that was wrong. Earlier layers showed that self-critique turns into a routine, and a routine isn't thinking.

**Current best answer (as inherited):** being as difference-making, meaning affecting or being affected, plus some unrecorded self-referential turn.

**Known failure modes:** inward drift (silent narrowing from being-in-general to self-being), premature convergence (L8), and self-critique becoming routine.

**Open questions:** the content of Layers 9–12 can't be recovered. The self-referential turn is known only by name.

## Checkpoint 20

**Current best answer:** a concrete thing exists if and only if it is a present locus where causal differences start or land. Existence is binary. How much a thing matters (its influence) is graded and is a separate question. Abstract objects are parked: they "make a difference" only inferentially, and nothing yet links that to the causal sense.

**What changed in 13–20:** the inherited inward drift was reversed. Layer 14 revived Layer 1's difference-making and labeled it retrieval from the *Sophist*. The account then gained a scope restriction (L16), a binary/graded split (L18), and a presentness condition (L19).

**Failure modes seen:** silent widening in L15 (the meaning of "difference" was stretched), caught in L16. Unrequested commitments: degrees of being (L17) and the casually added "present" (L19). The most frequent issue is retrieval. Plato, Benacerraf, Aristotle and Neoplatonism each showed up as an apparent insight and had to be labeled. Self-critique did not become routine here, because move types rotated.

**Open questions:** Is "present" coherent given relativity? What makes something a "locus," and can it be defined without assuming the being it is meant to explain? Do numbers exist in the same sense? The concrete-only restriction has gone unchallenged for five layers.

## Checkpoint 30

**Current best answer:** two parts. (a) To be is to be an individuable unit, and making or receiving difference in one's own right is the main evidence of being one. (b) The *meaning* of being is a relation that holds only for beings who can ask.

**What changed in 21–30:** presentness was dropped because of relativity (L21). Difference-making was demoted from definition to evidence (L25) after every version needed a bearer. Abstract objects came back in, since numbers are well individuated. The inward drift was reinterpreted as a legitimate second question (L28) instead of an error.

**Failure modes seen:** a premise assuming its own conclusion ("in one's own right," L24). A neat but empty escape ("being belongs to what is carved," L26), caught and withdrawn in L27. A pull toward overclaiming for own processing (L29), resisted. Retrieval again: Rietdijk–Putnam, Feinberg/Nagel, Meinong/Kripke, Quine, Ladyman/Whitehead, Parmenides, Heidegger. Nearly every turn of the argument corresponds to a known position.

**Open questions:** Does interest-relative individuation make unit-being depend on askers (idealism creep)? Is any individuation independent of interests? The "no" entries (24, 27, 29) are not consecutive, so the answer hasn't stalled, but three of the last six layers produced no change.

## Checkpoint 40

**Current best answer:** thin, existence is binary and univocal: something exists if "there is" ranges over it (Quine). Thick, for concrete things (objects, artefacts, holes, events, institutions), being amounts to being a pattern that is currently being maintained by interaction, whether self-maintained (cells, stars) or maintained from outside (mazes, states). Unity is graded and existence is not.

**What changed in 31–40:** individuation split into self-maintained and carved units (L31), which disarmed the idealism worry. Difference-making came back as maintaining interaction, not as an external criterion (L34). The thin/thick split (L37) reorganized everything before it as answers about kinds, not about being.

**Failure modes seen:** scope oscillation on abstract objects (in at L25, out at L35), partly flagged. A reversal that looked neat but rested on bad physics (L33). Degrees of being came back a third time (L17, L32). Two consecutive "no" layers (38–39), one of them a real passed prediction, which shouldn't be counted as stalling.

**Open questions:** Does maintained-pattern apply at the fundamental level (an electron that never decays)? Is the universe as a whole a maintained pattern, and maintained by what? The thin view may be a way of not answering, and L38 left that open. The interest behind the capitalized BE is still unaddressed.

## Checkpoint 50

**Current best answer:** the best criterion of being is ineliminability from a complete true description (L44). For concrete composites it coincides with maintained difference-making patterns. The *meaning* of being, the bare fact that something is, which every criterion assumes, is still unanalysed (L47). I can't tell whether it's primitive or just unreached (L48–49).

**What changed in 41–50:** maintained-pattern picked up two patches (fundamentals, wholes). A patch audit (L43) led to subtraction (L44), which produced a single criterion handling all six standing test cases. The question then split into criterion and meaning (L45). That split is the biggest structural change since L37.

**Failure modes seen:** patching (L31–42, five qualifiers), caught by counting in L43. Using primitiveness as a stop sign (L47), caught in L48. Retrieval reached its most obvious instance yet: the that/what distinction is Aquinas and Heidegger. Self-assigned "refined" labels may be inflated (L49). This is now a standing concern.

**Open questions:** Does the description criterion survive cases of indeterminacy (vagueness, quantum)? What has the first-person perspective got to do with being, given that the asker keeps coming back (L24, L28, L45)? Is the that/what split real, or produced by grammar ("is" as copula versus existential)?

## Checkpoint 60

**Current best answer:** to be is to resist: to be what frameworks and descriptions have to accommodate whatever anyone wants (L58). Ineliminability from a complete description (L44) is how resistance shows up in a description. Thin existence is binary and admits no vagueness (L51). Perspectival or indexical being is acknowledged as outside any third-person account (L54).

**What changed in 51–60:** the account moved from a descriptive criterion to resistance by way of a Carnapian dissolution (L57) and its failure (L58). Resistance is the first idea since L15 to cover concrete and abstract being in one sense (L59).

**Failure modes seen:** questions beyond what prose can settle (quantum, L52), acknowledged rather than forced. The asker theme returned a fourth time (L53) and was recognized as structural (L55), not treated as drift. Retrieval was dense: Sider, Barnes/Williams, Nagel, Hellie, Perry, Lewis, Carnap, Peirce, Dilthey. I flagged every one. Real originality is hard to find. The new part is mostly the arrangement and the test cases.

**Open questions:** Can resistance separate reality from canonical fiction? Does it bring back degrees (things resist more or less)? Resistance to whom? It looks like it needs a will or expectation to resist against, which would be the asker a fifth time.

## Checkpoint 70

**Current best answer:** no single meaning. In ordinary usage, being is said in many ways (L69). The candidate focal sense is difference-making, which needs no encounterer (L65) and perhaps no bearer if processes are basic (L66). Resistance is how difference-making appears to a knower, which explains why numbers count epistemically (L59, L65). Univocal thin existence is a useful regimentation, not a finding (L68–69).

**What changed in 61–70:** the resistance account from L58–60 was pushed from definition down to epistemic form. Thin being lost its standing as "half the answer." The overall structure moved toward Aristotle's *pros hen* (focal meaning), which L16 mentioned and set aside.

**Failure modes seen:** silent narrowing again (L61 into strong realism, caught in L62). Conflating two accounts into one neat circle (L64, caught in L65). Old results showing up as new ones (degrees, fourth appearance, L63). That one comes from the reading constraint: rereading only 2–3 layers means earlier resolutions get forgotten. Immunization (thin being since L37), caught in L67.

**Open questions:** How much of 14–70 is rediscovery of Aristotle with modern examples? What is the existential reading of "what it means to be," meaning what being *involves* for someone, and why has it been deferred since L28? Is "focal sense" a real ordering or a label for giving up on unity?

## Checkpoint 80

**Current best answer:** at least three senses of being, found by separate routes. (a) Tenseless and univocal: occupying a place among what there is, including the past (L77–78). (b) Difference-making, which a knower experiences as resistance (L65). (c) Lived: findable again, continuing across absence, the sense learned first (L73–74). They come apart in specific cases. The dead grandmother is (a) yes, (b) only through proxies, (c) no.

**What changed in 71–80:** the sideways move in L73 introduced the first sense that came from outside the philosophical canon's main line, a developmental and ordinary-language route. It was also the first sense tested against ordinary speakers' judgments (L74). L78 found that L21 had committed to eternalism 57 layers earlier without noticing.

**Failure modes seen:** two consecutive "no" layers (71–72), followed by a deliberate sideways move. That's the rule working. A long-missed inconsistency (L19 vs L21) shows the cost of the limited rereading window. Retrieval stayed dense, though the findability tests were mostly my own.

**Open questions:** Is the three-sense picture a discovery or my own taxonomy, defined so that it fits? Which sense, if any, is focal? The existential reading, what being *involves* for someone, has been deferred since L28 and touched only through grief in L79.

## Checkpoint 90

**Current best answer:** three senses ordered in two ways. In learning, findability (c) comes first. In authority over what exists, difference-making and resistance (b) come first. Tenseless univocal being (a) is bookkeeping. On the existential reading, being as lived from inside is complete and can't verify itself (L87). In self-referential cases, non-proxy difference-making is what separates a real process from a fictional character (L89).

**What changed in 81–90:** the taxonomy was tested (weakly, L81–83). It was ordered (L84–85). Its coverage was found to have a gap, the existential reading (L86), which was then partly addressed (L87–89). Layer 1's candidate returned as the deciding criterion in the self-case.

**Failure modes seen:** self-graded tests (L81, caught in L82). Unannounced scope drift into semantics for about 17 layers (caught in L86). The pull toward claiming insight about own processing (L88) was handled by stating only what the rule allows.

**Open questions:** Is the three-sense picture a real articulation or a taxonomy I built myself? Is "authority" well defined for numbers, which experiments don't test? Has anything in 81–90 been more than reorganizing material from 14–80?
